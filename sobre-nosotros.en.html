ABOUT DO FOR LOVE

Architecture, place, and city told through stories.

Do For Love is a digital magazine exploring architecture, urbanism, art, and the conflicts over land and place that transform our cities.

What is Do For Love?

Do For Love is an editorial platform telling stories of architecture beyond the building. We explore place, infrastructure, landscape, memory, urban conflict, and the ways cities shape everyday life.

Our mission

To make architecture, urbanism, and art accessible, engaging, and relevant to more people. Do For Love turns complex research into visual stories and editorial experiences that reach beyond specialist audiences.

What we publish

We publish place-based research, digital magazines, visual essays, maps, visual archives, data, and editorial work on cities, landscapes, culture, architecture, infrastructure, and the environment.

Our first dossier

Do For Love's first major project is ATOYAC: a place-based investigation into the environmental transformation of the Atoyac River and its relationship with water, industry, the city, and public health.
